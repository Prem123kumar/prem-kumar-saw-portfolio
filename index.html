Procurement & operations case studies

01 · Strategic sourcing · Supply continuity

Emergency sourcing during supply disruption

During a geopolitical supply disruption, the team contacted approximately 50–60 suppliers and secured 50 MT of material. Around 35 MT was received in the first five days, helping protect the customer production line. My contribution included supplier follow-up, alternate sourcing, emergency procurement coordination and production/customer communication.

02 · Cost optimisation · Alternate sourcing

PBT 30% granules — blended sourcing model

Compared a Bengaluru source at ₹300/kg with an alternate Mumbai source at ₹180/kg landed. By sourcing 2 MT from each supplier per month, the blended rate was ₹240/kg — a 20% reduction against the original rate, representing approximately ₹2.4 lakh monthly or ₹28.8 lakh annualised savings at the stated volume and specification.

03 · Logistics · Customer line protection

Expedited supply through air freight

When a Delhi supplier's dispatch was delayed, coordinated an emergency 2 MT air shipment covering approximately 10 days of demand. The supplier bore the freight cost, and the expedited material helped protect customer production continuity.

04 · Inventory · Supplier collaboration

Supplier-managed stock arrangement

Helped structure a replenishment model with two days of stock at the company (one working day plus one safety day) and three days maintained by suppliers. The arrangement supported a reported 25–30% reduction in excess inventory and wastage.
